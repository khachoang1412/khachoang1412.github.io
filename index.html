Make information count

I’m Khac-Hoang Ngo, an Assistant Professor and Docent in Electrical Engineering at Linköping University. I study how connected systems share information, learn, and make decisions in an efficient, reliable, and trustworthy manner.

  • Wireless communications · Information theory · AI privacy and security

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Chalmers and earned my PhD at CentraleSupélec, Paris-Saclay University. I'm a Vietnamese, married, with a little daughter. Full CV ↗

Connected intelligence.

Three connected directions, from the limits of communication to the security of learning systems.

01

Communication & distributed inference

What should a network deliver: every message, or the information we need?

I study how many devices can share information efficiently, even with limited coordination, energy, and knowledge of the wireless channel.

Explore this question ↗
02

Task-oriented sensing & transmission

Which parts of a 3D scene are worth transmitting?

I explore point-cloud transmission for remote registration: communicating with a downstream task in mind, rather than treating every bit as equally important.

Explore this question ↗
03

AI privacy & security

What can a learning system reveal, and what can it be made to remember?

I investigate privacy risks in learning systems and the foundations of attacks and defenses, including membership inference and memory poisoning in AI agents.

Explore this question ↗
